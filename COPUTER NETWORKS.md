### Proxy :
A **proxy** in networking is a **middleman** between your device and the internet.
an intermediary system that sits between a user's device and the internet, forwarding web requests and responses on the client's behalf
### What are the works they do ?
- **Hide your IP address** — the website sees the proxy’s IP, not yours.
    
- **Filter/block websites** — schools or offices can use a proxy to block certain sites.
    
- **Cache content** — save copies of websites to load them faster.
    
- **Bypass restrictions** — access content that may be blocked in your region.
    
- **Security/monitoring** — inspect or control traffic.

`Forward proxy`— sits in front of users/clients.
`Reverse proxy` — sits in front of servers, often used for load balancing and protection.
